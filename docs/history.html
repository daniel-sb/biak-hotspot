    Boundaries: desa/kelurahan (BIG, via this project's extraction). Hotspots: NASA FIRMS (VIIRS S-NPP, NOAA-20, NOAA-21, MODIS) and Himawari-9 AHI. A hotspot is a thermal anomaly, not a confirmed fire; it cannot identify who started anything. Confidence values are shown as raw instrument codes and are not comparable between VIIRS and MODIS.
