# index.html is permanently the 2026 season

The obvious arrangement for a fire site is a front page that always shows the
current season, with past seasons moving into a history page. We are not doing
that. `index.html` is the record of the August to September 2026 Biak season,
named as such in its title, and it does not rotate.

The reason is citation. The design-rationale paper points at this URL, and a
page that rewrites its own subject in 2027 breaks every citation to it after the
paper is published, at which point nothing can be done about it. If a later
season crosses a threshold stated in advance, it gets its own page rather than
overwriting this one.

The current state still reaches the reader, through the status strip described
in ADR 0001, which reports the last seven WIT days in three distinguishable
states: detections found, observed with none found, or stale and saying so.
