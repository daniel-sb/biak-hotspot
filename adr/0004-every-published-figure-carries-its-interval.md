# Every published figure carries its interval, on the front page

The site serves Biak readers and research reviewers equally rather than serving
one and relegating the other to a linked appendix. The consequence is that the
uncertainty apparatus cannot be hidden behind a link: it belongs beside the
number it qualifies, on the page a general reader lands on.

Burned area is the case that forces it. The dNBR+ product's mapped extent is
819 ha. The stratified estimate from the interpreted labels is 1,230 ha with a
1,060 to 1,342 ha interval, and the product's figure falls outside that interval
(F28, F29). The page therefore leads with the estimate, draws the interval
beneath it, and marks the mapped extent as a tick on that interval, visibly
outside it. A bare total appears nowhere on the site.

None of the five comparators does this. The Alaska Interagency Coordination
Center dashboard prints "Estimated Acres Burned This Year" as a single integer.
That gap is the design-rationale paper's stated contribution, so the display is
a claim being made, not a formatting preference.
