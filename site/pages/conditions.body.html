Drought context — monthly water balance

Rainfall alone does not describe drying. The upper panel is precipitation minus evapotranspiration: how much water the land actually gained or lost each month. The lower panel puts the rainfall against the 1981–2025 average for the same calendar month, so the reader can judge whether it was unusual rather than take the word for it.

Dry fuel makes burning easier. It does not start a fire, and this panel is not an explanation of why Biak burned — the hotspots above it were lit by someone. Wet months in this record contain hotspots too.

Vegetation indices are shown below with their composite-quality confound — see that panel for the correlation between observation quality and the index itself. Those indices cannot carry a drought claim here.

Vegetation indices and their composite quality

NDVI (MOD13A1) and NDMI (MOD09A1) departure from the 2001–2025 calendar-month mean, plotted with the share of AOI land pixels passing each product’s cloud test in the same composites. The index and its quality series share an x axis because the index tracks observation quality in the humid tropics: a dry month is also a less cloudy month, so its composite is built from more and better observations. These indices do not support a drought conclusion for this AOI.
